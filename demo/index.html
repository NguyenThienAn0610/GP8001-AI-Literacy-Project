    What do the scores mean?

    A model score is the probability assigned to a career label by the trained model. It is not your chance of success or a measure of your ability. Scores for these three suggestions may not add up to 100%, because the model considers 22 careers.

    In this dataset, interest is the strongest signal. A shortlist is more reliable than the first suggestion, and results have not been validated as real-world career advice.
